Delicious Bites Menu

Appetizers

  • Caprese Salad

    Fresh tomatoes, mozzarella, and basil drizzled with balsamic glaze.

  • Bruschetta

    Grilled or toasted baguette slices rubbed with garlic and topped with diced tomatoes, fresh basil, and often a drizzle of balsamic glaze.

  • Deviled Eggs

    Hard-boiled eggs halved, with yolks mashed and mixed with seasonings (like mustard or mayo), piped back into the whites, and optionally topped with crispy breadcrumbs or herbs.

Main Courses

  • Grilled Salmon

    Salmon fillet seasoned and grilled to perfection, served with lemon butter sauce.

  • Beef Wellington

    Tenderloin fillet coated with mushroom duxelles and wrapped in flaky puff pastry.

  • Seared Scallops

    Pan-seared sea scallops finished with a brown butter and lemon pan sauce.

Desserts

  • Chocolate Fondue

    Assorted fruits and marshmallows served with rich chocolate fondue.

  • Classic Tiramisu

    Espresso-soaked ladyfingers layered with whipped mascarpone cream and dusted with Dutch-process cocoa.

  • Seasonal Fruit Crisp or Cobbler

    Baked spiced apples or peaches under a buttery oat streusel, served warm.
